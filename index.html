About Us

Welcome to SLA Ghana

At Seed of Languages and Leadership Academy , accredited by NaSIA (National Schools Inspectorate Authority) , we believe that mastering a new language is the key to unlocking global opportunities. Located in the heart of Accra, we are an internationally recognized language and leadership institution serving Ghana and West Africa through a highly effective, student-centered learning environment with measurable outcomes and strong professional culture.
